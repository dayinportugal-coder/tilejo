Privacy Policy

Tilejo – free web version · Last updated: 25 October 2026 · Português

Tilejo is a free single-player puzzle game that runs in your web browser. It has no ads, no account and no analytics. Part of the game is free; the full game can optionally be unlocked with a licence key bought on Gumroad (section 4). Apart from that one licence check, the game sends nothing anywhere.

1. Who is responsible

Pedro Carvalho, Portugal. Contact: dayinportugal@gmail.com.

2. Data stored only in your browser

Your progress and settings (levels or puzzles completed, hints, streaks, language, sound and similar options) are saved in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB) on your device, so the game can remember them and work offline. This data is never sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings (this resets your progress).

3. Hosting

The game files are served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) or, if you play it there, by itch.io. Like any website, the host receives technical data needed to deliver the files, such as your IP address and browser type, and may keep it in security logs under its own privacy policy: GitHub Privacy Statement · itch.io Privacy Policy. We do not receive these logs.

4. Optional purchase: licence key check (Gumroad)

The full game is sold on Gumroad (Gumroad, Inc.). Payment happens on Gumroad's website, under the Gumroad Privacy Policy; the game never sees your card or payment details. As the seller, we receive from Gumroad the usual order details (such as your e-mail address and what you bought) and use them only to provide the product and support. When you type your licence key in the game, the game sends the key and the product identifier once to Gumroad (api.gumroad.com) to confirm the purchase, and, if you are online, re-checks it about every 14 days so refunded keys stop working. Your IP address is visible to Gumroad during that request, as with any website. The key and the "unlocked" status are then stored only in your browser's local storage. If you never enter a key, no request is made.

5. What we do not do

  • No ads and no advertising identifiers.
  • No payment data handled by the game.
  • No account, e-mail, contacts, location, microphone or camera.
  • No cookies of our own, no analytics or tracking, no selling or sharing of data.

6. Offline use and installation

After the first visit the game can be installed on your home screen and played offline. A service worker stores the game files in your browser's cache for that purpose only.

7. Children

The game is suitable for all ages and the game itself collects no personal data from anyone. Purchases on Gumroad are for adults (or with a parent's permission).

8. Your rights and changes

If you bought the full game, you can ask us to access, correct or delete the order details we received from Gumroad (some records may have to be kept for tax law). Everything else stays in your browser, under your control. For questions, write to dayinportugal@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Portuguese data protection authority (CNPD). If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
