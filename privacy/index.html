Privacy Policy

Tilejo – free web version · Last updated: 25 October 2026 · Português

Tilejo is a free single-player puzzle game that runs in your web browser. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no account and no analytics. We (the developer) do not collect, sell or receive any personal data from the game.

1. Who is responsible

Pedro Carvalho, Portugal. Contact: dayinportugal@gmail.com.

2. Data stored only in your browser

Your progress and settings (levels or puzzles completed, hints, streaks, language, sound and similar options) are saved in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB) on your device, so the game can remember them and work offline. This data is never sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings (this resets your progress).

3. Hosting

The game files are served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) or, if you play it there, by itch.io. Like any website, the host receives technical data needed to deliver the files, such as your IP address and browser type, and may keep it in security logs under its own privacy policy: GitHub Privacy Statement · itch.io Privacy Policy. We do not receive these logs.

4. What we do not do

  • No ads and no advertising identifiers.
  • No purchases or payment data.
  • No account, e-mail, contacts, location, microphone or camera.
  • No cookies of our own, no analytics or tracking, no selling or sharing of data.

5. Offline use and installation

After the first visit the game can be installed on your home screen and played offline. A service worker stores the game files in your browser's cache for that purpose only.

6. Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

7. Your rights and changes

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete; your local data is under your control in your browser. For questions, write to dayinportugal@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Portuguese data protection authority (CNPD). If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
